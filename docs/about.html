浅觉深知（Praxis Advisory）2023年成立于上海。我们的愿景：让更多企业——而不只是行业头部——能够触达世界顶级的管理咨询，并让它真正创造价值。 Founded in Shanghai in 2023. Our vision: make world-class management consulting accessible to more companies — not just industry leaders — and turn it into real value.

我们的故事Our Story

为什么创办浅觉深知Why We Founded Praxis Advisory

过去，顶级管理咨询几乎是头部企业的专属。高昂的费用、金字塔式的项目团队、漫长的交付周期——对大多数企业来说，麦肯锡式的咨询方法只能仰望，难以触达。 Top-tier consulting was long reserved for industry leaders. High fees, pyramid-shaped teams, long delivery cycles — for most companies, McKinsey-style methods were something to admire, not to access.

我们成立浅觉深知，就是要改变这件事：让更多企业——无论规模——都能用上世界级的方法论，把战略、定价与增长真正变成结果。 We founded Praxis Advisory to change that: to put world-class methodology within reach of more companies, whatever their size — and turn strategy, pricing and growth into real outcomes.

AI 的到来，让这件事第一次变得可能。我们用 AI 原生的方式重新组织咨询工作——更快的分析、更低的交付成本、更透明的过程——把省下来的资源，投入在真正创造价值的地方。 AI made this possible for the first time. We organize consulting the AI-native way — faster analysis, lower delivery cost, more transparent process — reinvesting what we save into what actually creates value.
