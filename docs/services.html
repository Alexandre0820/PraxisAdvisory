合作模式Engagement Models

从轻到重，灵活匹配From Light to Heavy — a Model for Every Stage

无论是一天的培训、半年陪跑，还是完整的咨询项目，我们都用同一套方法论标准交付。From a one-day workshop to a six-month co-pilot to a full consulting engagement — delivered with the same methodological rigor.

模式一 · 定制化完整项目Model 1 · Full Custom Engagement

目标与交付物明确，项目周期约4–12周。适用于市场研究、出海策略、AI数字化战略等。Clear objectives and deliverables, typically 4–12 weeks. Market research, go-global strategy, AI & digital strategy.

模式二 · 长期辅导与陪跑Model 2 · Long-term Co-piloting

目标明确但周期灵活，通常6个月以上，导师可常驻或兼职。适用于管理体系提升、定价体系优化、AI应用陪跑。Clear goals, flexible timeline — typically 6+ months with embedded or part-time advisors. Management systems, pricing systems, AI co-piloting.

模式三 · 轻量化培训Model 3 · Lightweight Training

按天计，先建立认知与技能，企业团队自行落地。适用于AI培训、销售培训、数字化转型启动。Day-based, building awareness and skills your team applies independently. AI training, sales training, digital transformation kickoffs.
