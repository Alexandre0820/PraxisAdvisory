战略案例Strategy Cases

汽车 · 战略Auto · Strategy经销商绩效提升Dealer Performance

某欧洲车企 · 中国经销商网络绩效提升European OEM · Dealer Network Performance in China

挑战：经销商网络整体盈利承压，单店业绩与利润参差不齐，商务政策与终端销售动作脱节。Challenge: A dealer network under margin pressure, with wide performance gaps across stores and weak links between commercial policy and frontline execution.

方法：对全网经销商做绩效诊断与分层，建立关键指标对标体系；重构商务政策与目标设定机制；在试点门店导入销售与库存管理工具，并驻场辅导。Approach: Performance diagnosis and segmentation across the network, a KPI benchmarking system, redesigned commercial policies, and on-site coaching with sales and inventory tools at pilot stores.

试点门店业绩与盈利显著提升，形成可复制的经销商管理工具包并向全网推广。Pilot stores delivered significant gains in sales and profitability; the resulting dealer-management toolkit was rolled out network-wide.
出行 · 战略Mobility · Strategy市场评估与新服务上市Market Assessment & Launch

某德国车企 · 移动出行市场评估与服务上市German OEM · Mobility Market Assessment & Service Launch

挑战：出行市场正从"卖车"转向"卖服务"，管理层需要判断是否进入、以及以何种方式进入。Challenge: With mobility shifting from selling cars to selling services, leadership needed a clear go/no-go and entry model.

方法：出行市场容量与竞争格局评估、目标客群细分、服务模式与定价设计、试点城市选择与分阶段上市路径。Approach: Market sizing and competitive mapping, customer segmentation, service-model and pricing design, pilot-city selection, and a phased launch roadmap.

形成清晰的进入判断、服务组合与上市计划，直接支撑董事会决策。Delivered a clear entry decision, service portfolio and launch plan that directly informed board-level decisions.
制造 · 转型Industrial · Transformation重组路线图Restructuring Roadmap

某中法合资企业 · 重组路线图Sino-French JV · Restructuring Roadmap

挑战：业务与组织双重承压，管理层与股东对"往哪走、怎么走"存在分歧。Challenge: Pressure on both business and organization, with management and shareholders divided on direction and pace.

方法：业务组合与成本结构全面诊断，设计聚焦与退出方案；明确组织调整与流程再造；制定分阶段实施路线图与治理决策机制。Approach: Full diagnostic of portfolio and cost structure, focus and exit options, organizational and process redesign, and a phased roadmap with clear governance.

形成各方认可的重组路线图，明确未来12–24个月的关键里程碑与资源安排。A restructuring roadmap endorsed by all parties, with milestones and resource plans for the next 12–24 months.

某中国汽车集团 · 欧洲车企战略投资商业尽调Chinese Auto Group · Cross-border Investment Due Diligence

完整的商业尽调结论，为投资决策与谈判策略提供依据。A complete commercial due-diligence conclusion informing the investment decision and negotiation strategy.

02 — 定价与卓越销售Pricing & Sales Excellence

定价与销售案例Pricing & Sales Cases

软件 · 定价Software · Pricing定价转型 · 三年陪跑Pricing Transformation · 3-Year Co-pilot

某中国开源软件企业 · 定价转型与提价Chinese Open-Source Firm · Pricing Transformation & Price Increase

挑战：开源生态下的商业变现路径不清晰，价格体系缺失，增长依赖融资而非收入。Challenge: An unclear monetization path in an open-source ecosystem, no price architecture, and growth funded by capital rather than revenue.

方法：从"技术价值"重新定义"客户价值"，设计分层产品与价格体系；分阶段实施提价与商业化路径；陪跑三年，持续校准定价、销售与产品策略。Approach: Reframed customer value beyond technology, built a tiered product-and-price architecture, executed phased price increases, and co-piloted for three years to keep pricing, sales and product aligned.

三年内收入实现100%增长，商业模式从融资驱动转向收入驱动。Revenue doubled within three years, shifting the business from capital-driven to revenue-driven growth.
消费品 · 定价Consumer · Pricing提价与利润恢复Price Increase & Margin Recovery

某美国鞋类企业 · 提价与利润恢复US Footwear Company · Price Increase & Margin Recovery

挑战：成本上涨持续挤压利润，直接提价则担心渠道与终端抵触。Challenge: Rising costs squeezing margins, with fears that direct price increases would trigger channel and consumer pushback.

方法：价格带与SKU利润池分析，制定分层定价策略；设计分渠道提价执行计划与销售话术；同步优化经销商库存与返利结构。Approach: Price-band and SKU profit-pool analysis, tiered pricing, a channel-by-channel increase plan with sales playbooks, and rebalanced distributor rebates and inventory terms.

项目实现利润率提升3–5%，且未损失市场份额。3–5% margin uplift without losing market share.
汽车 · 定价Auto · Pricing新品定价与上市Launch Pricing

某日本汽车OEM · 新品定价与上市策略Japanese Auto OEM · New Product Launch Pricing

挑战：新车型上市前价格定位模糊，缺乏与竞品的价值对标，内部对定价存在分歧。Challenge: Unclear price positioning before a model launch, no structured value benchmark against competitors, and internal disagreement on pricing.

方法：客户价值研究（VOC）与竞品价格对标，构建价值-价格定位；设计配置定价与价格带；上市前进行价格测试并建立经销商反馈机制。Approach: Voice-of-customer research and competitor benchmarking to build a value-price position, configuration pricing and price bands, pre-launch price testing, and a dealer feedback loop.

新车定价获得经销商与终端认可，上市初期订单符合预期。Launch pricing was embraced by dealers and customers; early orders met expectations.
半导体 · 销售Semiconductors · Sales销售培训 · 10场Sales Training · 10 Sessions

某新加坡半导体公司 · 销售培训体系Singapore Semiconductor Firm · Sales Training Program

挑战：销售团队打法不统一，新市场拓展乏力，年度业绩目标压力大。Challenge: Inconsistent sales playbooks, weak traction in new markets, and aggressive annual targets.

方法：定制销售方法论与客户分层打法；全年交付10场实战培训，覆盖拜访、谈判、关键客户管理与销售工具落地；训后跟进辅导与复盘机制。Approach: A custom sales methodology with customer-tiering playbooks, ten hands-on training sessions across prospecting, negotiation, key account management and tooling, plus post-training coaching and reviews.

帮助团队超额完成2025年度业绩目标。The team exceeded its 2025 revenue targets.
